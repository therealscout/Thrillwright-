# How to play Thrillwright (2026-11-07)

Thrillwright is a theme park builder. You start with a field, some money and a goal. Lay paths, open rides and stalls, design roller coasters, and keep your guests happy enough that more of them come.

## Getting started

1. Open the game and pick **First Steps Fairground** from the scenario list. It walks you through everything below.
2. Each park has a **goal** (the ◎ chip in the top bar) and a deadline: the end of October in a given year. Meet every part of the goal in time to win and unlock the next park.
3. The game saves itself every month and whenever you leave the page. Press **Ctrl+S** or the 💾 button to save right away, and use **Continue** on the menu to carry on.

## Building

- **Paths (5):** guests only walk on paths. Every ride and stall needs its entrance facing a path.
- **Rides & stalls (B):** pick from the menu and click to place. Press **R** to turn the entrance.
- **Coaster builder (C):** place a three-tile station on flat land, then add pieces from the builder panel, or press **Build sample**. Test the coaster to see its excitement, intensity and nausea, then open it.
- **Land (1–4, 7):** raise and lower land, add and drain water, and buy more land next to the park.
- **Bins and benches (8, 9):** click a path to place them.
- **Undo / redo:** Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y.

## Keeping guests happy

- Guests get hungry, thirsty, tired and in need of the restrooms. Build food, drink, restrooms and benches.
- They have opinions. Click the ★ rating to see what they're thinking and what's pushing your rating up or down.
- Litter and sick make paths dirty. Hire **handymen** to sweep and add bins.
- Rides wear out and break down. Hire **mechanics** to inspect and fix them.
- Prices matter. Guests won't pay much more for a ride than it's worth to them.

## Running the business (Manage)

- **Finances:** monthly accounts, a profit chart, loans, and the park entry fee. Charge more than the park is worth and fewer people come.
- **Marketing:** flyers, radio and TV campaigns bring in more visitors.
- **Research:** fund the lab to discover new rides, stalls and coaster pieces (banked turns and the loop).

## Time and weather

- Pause with **P**, or use the 1×, 2× and 3× buttons in the top bar.
- Days turn to night once a month, and the weather changes every few minutes. Rain keeps some visitors at home.

## Options (⚙)

- Sound: mute, music, effects, crowd and weather.
- Display: day and night, and rain and clouds.
- Graphics: High, Medium or Low (Low turns off shadows for older computers).
- Accessibility: interface size (100% to 150%) and reduced motion.

## Camera

- **W A S D**, the arrow keys, or right-drag to move
- **Q / E** to rotate
- Scroll or **+ / −** to zoom
